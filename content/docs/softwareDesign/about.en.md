# Software Design for Agile Development

In agile development, the required software specifications keep changing throughout development.
That is why we need a "flexible" design that is resilient to change.

To be able to design and implement flexibly in a way that is resilient to change, it is extremely important to know the principles of object-oriented programming, such as the SOLID principles and design patterns, and to be able to put them to use.

Compared to the earlier chapter on software testing, the explanations that follow are harder and drier,
but this chapter covers the most important content that an agile developer should master.

This chapter gives only a "taste" of the SOLID principles and related topics.
Entire books have been published on this area, so it is difficult to cover everything here.

If this chapter makes you want to take on agile development, please read ["Clean Architecture"](https://asciidwango.jp/post/176293765750/clean-architecture).
You are sure to learn a great deal from it.
