# What Is a Developer

Developers (Devs) are responsible for implementing the backlog and building the product. They also give input on technical aspects when solutions are being considered, playing a part in the team's understanding and decision-making.
The basic approach to development follows [Twelve-Factor](https://12factor.net/ja/).

Within a balanced team, they contribute to the product's success by bringing the perspective of ***Feasible (technical feasibility)*** through the Extreme Programming methodology.
