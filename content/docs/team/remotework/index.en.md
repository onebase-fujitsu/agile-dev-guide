# Remote Work

In remote work, the main means of communication is online.
Because people do not see each other face to face when communicating online,
the psychological distance between members grows and the barrier to small talk rises.
Therefore, to communicate smoothly
and build a team with high psychological safety, the following are needed.
- Setting rules
- Using effective tools
- Running initiatives specific to remote work

## Rules

### Rules for Team Meetings
- Mute yourself when you are not speaking
- Turn your video on
- If you leave or return during a session, let others know
- As needed, use Zoom's chat feature to ask to speak or to ask questions
- Avoid side conversations, as they are distracting
- Set clear goals and stick to the agenda schedule
- Create an agenda on the fly as needed
- Stay focused: put small talk and things you want to check later in the "parking lot," and have side conversations at the appropriate time
- Balance speaking time to improve collaboration and understanding

## Initiatives
- Stimulating communication
  - Ice Break
    - Small talk using Spatial Chat
    - Online board games
- Securing a place and time to ease worries and consult with each other
  - Internal Sync
  - 1on1
- Physical refreshment
	- A 10–15 minute break every 1 to 1.5 hours
	- Radio Taiso after lunch break
