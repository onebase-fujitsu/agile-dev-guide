# Radio Taiso

## Overview
Radio Taiso is a set of calisthenics performed to musical accompaniment, devised to improve physical fitness and maintain and promote health.  
In Japan, it is widely loved by the public as an exercise that anyone can enjoy casually.

## Purpose
By moving our bodies and refreshing ourselves, we maintain the team's concentration and the quality of our work.
- Relieving stiff shoulders
- Improving posture

## Frequency and Timing
- Optional (e.g., every day after lunch break)

## How to Run It
- The whole team does Radio Taiso together while playing a video on a site such as YouTube
