# 1on1

## Overview
Team members talk with each other one-on-one.
Rather than a career-oriented 1on1 between a manager and a direct report, this is a casual conversation with a teammate you work closely with.

## Purpose
Through 1on1s, members share what went well and what they are worried about, ask for advice on their concerns, and offer new insights.

## Frequency and Timing
- 1–2 times a month, about 15–30 minutes
- Held as needed according to the wishes and circumstances of the members, without being bound by a fixed frequency

## How to Run It
- Schedule it while taking into account pair assignments and the work in progress
- Secure a place where you can talk one-on-one, such as Zoom or a meeting space
- Hold the 1on1 meeting and clear your mind
  > **Example topics**
  > - Small talk
  > - Thoughts about the team
  > - Recent concerns
  > - How you want to grow through the project
