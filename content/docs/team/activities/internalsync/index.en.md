# Internal Sync

## Overview
Internal Sync is a meeting held only among members from within the company.
Members share what they learned from the day's work, clear up open questions, and discuss future policies and ways of working.

## Purpose
When there are things that are hard to talk about in front of members from outside the company, this meeting provides psychological safety for sharing and consulting.
It is also used as time to summarize learnings for internal reporting.

## Frequency and Timing
- Once a day, about 30–60 minutes
- Because it takes time away from pair work, hold it at a frequency and timing the team agrees on

## How to Run It
- The participating members decide how to run it
