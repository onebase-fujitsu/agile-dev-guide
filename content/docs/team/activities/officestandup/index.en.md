# Office Standup

## Overview
A meeting held standing up, in which all the teams working in the same workplace gather.
The following are shared:
- NewFaces (new members)
- Interestings (things that are interesting or of interest)
- Helps (things you are struggling with or want help with)
- Events (today's events)

## Purpose
Create a daily work rhythm together with the members working in the same workplace.
It also provides an opportunity for communication beyond team boundaries.

## Frequency and Timing
- Every morning, about 5–10 minutes

## How to Run It
### Preparation
- Decide on a host
- Post what you want to share on Slack

### Meeting
- The host goes through the items posted in advance in order
  - NewFaces: New members introduce themselves
  - Interestings: The poster explains the item
  - Helps: The poster explains what they are struggling with, and the host checks on the spot whether anyone can take action
  - Events: The poster shares the event
- Close with a single ceremonial clap (ippon-jime)

{{% hint info %}}
**Tips**
- Keep it quick so that it does not take more than 10 minutes
- Feel free to raise things in Interestings or Helps even if they are not directly related to work (even small things may be fresh to team members)
{{% /hint %}}
