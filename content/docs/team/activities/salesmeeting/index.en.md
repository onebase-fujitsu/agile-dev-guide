# Sales Sync Meeting

## Overview
The sales sync meeting is a forum for sharing information with the sales team and other people who serve as points of contact for each end user.
When necessary, the team also asks the sales team for help, such as with research.

## Purpose
By sharing information regularly, we build a cooperative relationship aimed at the success of the project.
People who serve as points of contact for end users need accurate information about the timing and content of product releases, as well as the direction of the product.
Also, hearing the voices of end users, even on topics the team has not researched directly, can lead to new discoveries and become the seeds of the next hypotheses.

## Frequency and Timing
- Once a week, 30 minutes
- Held when close collaboration with the sales team is needed, such as for a certain period up to the first release or around releases

## How to Run It
- Updates from the agile team
- Updates from the sales team / end-user contacts
