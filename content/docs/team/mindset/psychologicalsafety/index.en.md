# Psychological Safety

Ensuring "psychological safety" is extremely important in carrying out a project.
"Psychological safety" refers to a state in which team members can speak and act with peace of mind, without being rejected or punished for voicing ideas, questions, concerns, mistakes, and so on.
If a high level of "psychological safety" can be maintained, the following benefits can be gained.
- Communication becomes more active and team performance improves
- A positive mindset forms and improvement activities become more active
- People become more willing to take an interest in what is around them, creating more opportunities for growth
