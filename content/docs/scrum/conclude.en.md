# Summary

So far, we have looked at Scrum's roles, the artifacts a Scrum team should create, and the flow of development in a Scrum team.
It is important that the product owner and the entire product development team understand what has been explained here.

In addition, to get good results with Scrum, you need to adopt and put into practice the five Scrum values.

* **Commitment**
  * Each person commits to doing their utmost to achieve the goal
* **Courage**
  * Have the courage to do the right thing and work on tough problems
* **Focus**
  * Everyone focuses on the work of the sprint and on achieving the goal
* **Openness**
  * Agree to be open about all the work and problems
* **Respect**
  * Respect each other as capable individuals

It is important that the whole Scrum team not merely carry out the framework as written,
but act based on these five values.
