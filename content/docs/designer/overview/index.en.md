# What Is a Designer

Designers deliver value in the form of design decisions.
Their job is to deeply understand users in order to define solutions that are desirable and easy to use for them.
Working with the Product Manager, they validate assumptions about users and solutions in combination with user research.

Within a balanced team, they contribute to the product's success by bringing the perspective of ***Desirable (user value)*** through user-centered design methodologies.
