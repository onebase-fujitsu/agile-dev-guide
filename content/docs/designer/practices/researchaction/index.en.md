# Conducting Research

## Overview

In research, user interviews, user tests, and the like are conducted to obtain information for validating hypotheses.

The following work is carried out:
- Conduct the research according to the script
- The note-taker records what is said (in a Google spreadsheet)

## Key Points of Conducting Research

- If you will share screens or record, get consent for that
- Don't jump straight into the main topic; keep in mind building a good relationship with the participant (rapport)
- There is no need to be overly formal
- When having participants try a prototype, tell them that it is the UI being evaluated, not the participants themselves
- Don't over-explain the UI. Observe how the participant operates it
- Don't lead the participant's opinions
- As a third party, you may dig into things that the client finds hard to ask. Striking the right balance is important
- Research sessions last at most one hour
- Tips for user testing are compiled in books on human-centered design, so read them
